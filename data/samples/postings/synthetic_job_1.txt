Senior NLP / GenAI Engineer
Company: CognitiveScale AI
Location: Remote (US / EMEA)
Employment Type: Full-time

About the Role:
We are seeking an experienced Senior NLP / GenAI Engineer to join our core AI engineering team. You will architect, optimize, and deploy autonomous LLM agents and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines in production.

Responsibilities:
- Build and evaluate agentic AI workflows with multi-step reasoning, tool use, and structured outputs.
- Fine-tune open-weights models and develop RAG architectures utilizing dense retrieval and vector indexes.
- Scale real-time inference microservices using FastAPI, Docker, and Kubernetes.
- Collaborate with product and ML teams to implement automated hallucination guardrails and evaluation harnesses.

Required Qualifications (Must-Have):
- Strong expertise in Python with asynchronous programming (asyncio).
- Production experience with Large Language Models (LLMs) and generative AI architectures.
- Hands-on mastery of deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch or Hugging Face Transformers.
- Practical experience designing RAG systems and using vector databases (e.g., FAISS, Qdrant, Pinecone, or Chroma).
- Solid foundation in RESTful API development using FastAPI or Flask.
- Experience with Git version control and modern CI/CD deployment pipelines.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Have):
- Experience deploying models on Kubernetes and AWS cloud infrastructure.
- Familiarity with LangChain, LlamaIndex, or custom LCEL architectures.
- Understanding of prompt optimization, quantization (e.g. bitsandbytes, AWQ, GGUF), and LoRA fine-tuning.
- Familiarity with semantic search and re-ranking algorithms (e.g., Cohere, Cross-Encoders).

Hard Requirements & Deal-Breakers:
- 3+ years professional software or machine learning engineering experience in Python.
- Proven track record of deploying at least one GenAI or NLP service in a live production environment.
